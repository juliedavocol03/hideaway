HIDEAWAY

A place worth finding.

In the heart of San Juan, Siquijor, Hideaway was created to bring something new to the island — an enclosed, elevated restaurant and bar that feels luxurious without feeling out of reach.

A place where food, cocktails, design, and atmosphere come together — giving locals and travelers a reason to stay a little longer.

A market

That is 62% above 2019, showing continued demand for hospitality, dining, and nightlife.
